**Camera numbering.** The image folders cam1–cam4 are cameras 0–3 in the detection tables and in the GUI's files. I take them to be C1–C4 of the photo, which fits the GUI's window assignment.

**What the synthetic tests say about this rig.** They found this kind of rig reliable:
- two cameras on each wall;
- views of 7–10°;
- walls about 90° apart, the angle that test A1 found the most precise;
- thousands of frames.

**What the synthetic tests never covered:**
- real detection errors (the centres of 26–45 px blobs);
- lens distortion;
- a wall thickness or refractive index that differs from the value given.

**The focal length.** The fit estimates f from the data and needs no starting value. A 180 mm lens focused at 0.6–0.8 m has an image distance of about 230–250 mm. With 13.5 µm pixels that is f ≈ 17,000–19,000 px, not 13,333. So the GUI's f, held near 13,333 px, may be well off, and T7 checks this.
