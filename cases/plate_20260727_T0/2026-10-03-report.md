## 3. A crash in the calibration, fixed

**The crash:** with more than about 550 frames, the first fits stopped with an IndexError in
`cylinder_wand.affine_factorization`. That is the start-up stage that both the plate and the cylinder calibration use.
- **Cause:** its outlier rejection runs at most 5 rounds. When the last round still rejected frames, it returned a
  frame list that no longer matched its factorisation.
- **Why only now:** real detections with a few gross errors keep it rejecting to the end; synthetic data never did.
- **Fix:** return the frames that the last factorisation used. It is two lines in `cylinder_wand.py`, made with your
  approval.

**Checks:**

  run converged or crashed. The test data crash the old code and pass the fixed code

## 6. Wrong minima: the main thing to watch

**How often:** from scratch, with no hints, the calibration ended in a wrong minimum on:
- **k1 fitted:** 2 of the 9 frame sets tried: the second half of the recording, and every frame (T2's first run);
- **k1 held:** 1 of 8 (subset s0). The 4 T5/T6 variants on s0's frames ended in the same wrong minimum;
- **with the edge filter:** 1 of 2 (s0).

There are two kinds:
- **Kind A:** k1 held, subset s0; k1 fitted with the edge filter, subset s0; and every frame (T2's first run).
  - **Both walls are wrong:** the walls are 93.5–94.1° apart, and every camera sits only 0.19–0.31 m from its wall. C1
    is at 16° to its wall instead of 0.7°.
  - **Residuals:** RMS 0.58–0.65 px. The wand length error on unused frames is 0.020 mm, three times the right
    answer's.
  - **The same answer each time:** the three runs end with the same walls 93.5–94.1° apart and the same cameras.
- **Kind B:** k1 fitted, the second half.
  - **Wall 2 is tilted the other way:** C3 and C4 swap their angles to it (2° and 19° instead of 18° and 1–2°) and sit
    0.2 m from it.
  - **Residuals:** the walls' angle stays at 90.14°. RMS 0.55 px. The wand length error on unused frames is
    0.032 mm, five times the right answer's.

**How they are reached:** stages 1–3 build two starts.
- **Poor starts:** on these frames both starts are far off: 30 px RMS after stage 3, against 1.5–2.6 px on the good
  subsets. The two walls' camera pairs each fit well alone (0.2–0.4 px); joining the two pairs goes wrong.
- **The rest is luck:** which minimum stage 4 then reaches is chance. The plain s0 fit with k1 was freed by the
  perturbed restarts, which cut its cost by 48 %.
- **One worse choice:** in one case a start reached 0.43 px but was not kept. The start selection (`_choose`) keeps the
  answer with more detections among those within 1.5× of the best RMS, and kept the 0.58 px one.

**Why this rig:** wall 2 is seen only by C3 and C4, under 5.3–6.0° with their real f (about 21,500 px).
- **The 6° limit:** the synthetic tests found two-camera windows failing below 6°
  (`tests/plate_wand/2026-10-03-when-calibration-fails.md`). The report's `weak_window` warning fires for some frame
  sets of this rig.
- **Wall 1:** C1 and C2 see the wand under about 7°.

**The warnings do not catch them reliably.** The "noisy" warning (sigma above 0.5 px) flagged 2 of the 4: sigma 0.56
and 0.502 px. It missed the other two, at sigma 0.47 and 0.495 px.

**How to tell them apart:**
- **RMS:** about 0.27 px when right, 0.55 px or more when wrong.
- **The cameras' distances and angles to their walls:**

  | camera | distance to its wall | angle to its wall's normal |
  |---|---|---|
  | C1 | about 1.07 m | 0.7° |
  | C2 | about 1.03 m | 12.8° |
  | C3 | 0.75–0.78 m | 18° |
  | C4 | 0.83–0.85 m | 1–2° |

- **The walls' angle:** about 90.15° when right. This catches kind A only.

**How to avoid them:** start from a known right answer (`run_calibration.py --start`, or
`plate_wand.calibrate(start=...)`). The second half then converged to the right answer in 102 s instead of 37 min.
Hints: section 8.

## 7. The walls' positions and the "pinned" warning (D1–D3)

**What the warning means:** most fits end with the report's `pinned` warning. A wand end lies exactly on wall 1's
water-side surface (C1 and C2's wall).

**With k1 held at 0, the warning is a real model error.** The walls absorb the lens distortion, and the fit pushes
wall 1 into the wand cloud. When D1 re-solved such a fit, a wand end ended 4.8 mm inside the acrylic.

**With k1 fitted, it comes from a few bad frames** (D2, `tests/d2_pinned_frames_t3k_s4_out.txt`).
- **Which frames:** 7 frames of subset s4 whose wand came within about 1 mm (in the model) of the C1/C2 wall. In each,
  the near-wall ball is cut by C3's or C4's left image border, so its centre is biased (`tests/d2_pinned_*.png`).
- **The fit is stuck there.** A step that would push those ends into the wall is refused. Holding wall 1 2–5 mm closer
  to its cameras and refitting lowers the cost by 41 px² on s0, about delta chi² = 1,500 (D1,
  `tests/d1_wall_offsets_t3k_s0_out.txt`).
- **The price:** C1's and C2's f are about 0.5 % low in the pinned fits, and wall 1 is 2–5 mm off.
- **The geometry is plausible:** the near-wall ball centres sit 0–10 mm from the wall in the fits, and the balls are
  1.5–2 mm in radius. So the wand probably touched, or nearly touched, the C1/C2 wall in those frames.

**Dropping the border-cut balls is not the answer** (D3, `tests/d3_edge_compare_out.txt`). Dropping the 697 border-cut
detections removes the pin (s4).
- **The rig moves:** wall 2 shifts by 30 mm and C1's f by 0.6 %.
- **The measurement does not:** on unused frames the wand length error is 0.0070 mm against 0.0067 mm without the
  filter.
- **T2 does not use it.** The filter is an option of the case (`--edge-margin`).

**The walls' positions are the weakest part of this calibration.**
- **The statistics look tight:** D1 with k1 fitted gives sigmas of 1.3 mm for wall 1 and 0.6 mm for wall 2
  (`tests/d1_wall_offsets_t3k_s4_out.txt`).
- **But they move between frame sets:** 20 mm between the halves, and 30 mm with the edge filter. Their information
  comes from the third-order refraction effect, which is largest at the image edges, where the detections are least
  reliable.
- **So:** the cameras' distances to the walls are uncertain by a few cm.
- **The measurement inside the wand's volume does not depend on them.** D1 moved each wall by ±10–20 mm and refitted;
  the wand length error on unused frames stayed at 0.0066–0.0069 mm.
