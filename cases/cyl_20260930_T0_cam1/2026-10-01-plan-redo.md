# Redo of the cam1 calibration with the corrected inputs: plan

Date: 2026-10-01. Status: **approved by the user on 2026-10-01 and carried out**; results in
`2026-10-01-report.md`. The deviations are explained in its section 7:
- the delivered model is M1bx, M1b plus a sideways shift of each plane (the stage's play), found in the M1b
  residuals;
- T5's choice rule gained a tie margin, and lens distortion was excluded;
- T4 fails as specified, because the training fit removes the stage play that the unseen planes keep.

The first calibration and its approved plan are `2026-09-30-report.md` and `2026-09-30-plan.md`; this plan changes
only what the corrected inputs require.

| input | first run | corrected (user, 2026-10-01) |
|---|---|---|
| plane spacing | 2 mm per step (file labels `z_0mm` ... `z_38mm`) | **2.54 mm (0.1 in) per step**: plane k sits at z = 2.54 k mm, 0 ... 48.26 mm; the file labels stay as they are |

The detection and indexing do not depend on any of these: the size gates are relative to the median dot, and the
synthetic dots of T2 take their size from the images. The dots and their (i, j) indices therefore stay exactly as
they are. The diameter enters only T6 (the circle-centre bias).

## 2. What the data already say (read-only probes, before this plan)

Run from the scratchpad on the saved first-run detections and fit state; nothing in the case or the outputs changed.

1. **The stage steps are regular, and f is about 41,400 px.** The vertical dot pitch runs along the tube axis, so
   the tube's curvature hardly affects it. It grows from 115.37 px (plane 0) to 119.40 px (plane 19), +3.49 %.
   - Against z = 2.54 k it is linear to 0.006 px rms, so the steps are regular to about 0.07 mm or better.
   - A straight-line fit of 1/pitch against z gives f = 41,393 px, with no cylinder model. The first calibration
     fitted 41,416 px with free plane positions.
   - The pinhole of the model (the lens's entrance pupil) is then about 1.08 m from plane 0, measured as an
     equivalent distance in air.
   - With the pinhole at the lens front (about 20 cm from the tube), the pitch would grow by 14.5 %; at the camera
     body (about 36.5 cm from the tube), by 8.7 %.
   - The focus ring sets where the entrance pupil is and the effective f, so the calibration holds for this focus
     setting only.
2. **Fixing the planes at 2.54 k (model M1b: the first run's M2b without the free plane offsets):**
   - the fit rms goes from 0.252 to 0.304 px;
   - f is 41,424 px and the camera is 1093 mm from the axis, both unchanged;
   - the axis moves to X = 20.9 mm (from 19.3 mm).
3. **Held-out planes, predicted at their stage position.** I trained on the even planes and predicted the odd ones
   at z = 2.54 k:

   | model | held-out rms (px) | worst plane (px) |
   |---|---|---|
   | M1b, fixed planes | 0.343 | 0.61 |
   | M2b, free planes (first run) | 0.522 | 0.93 |

   Fitting each held-out plane's own z gives 0.28 px for both. The free plane positions scatter by 0.17 mm per step
   because they absorb model error; they fit the training planes better but predict real positions worse.
4. **One free scale on the stage steps.** The rms barely changes between 1.00 and 1.02: 0.3037 px at 1.00, a
   minimum of 0.3010 px at 1.01. The data agree with 2.54 mm steps to about 1 %. That is within the tube constants'
   tolerances: by T7, n_water +0.005 or R_outer -1.1 mm would account for 1 %.
5. **Still undetermined: the traverse direction.** Freeing the traverse tilt gives a tilt of 6 degrees and moves the
   camera 121 mm sideways, for 0.003 px. The board is therefore assumed square to its travel, as in the first run.
6. **The diagnostic wall field** (not exportable to pyOpenLPT) still helps: fit 0.253 px, held-out 0.296 px.

## 3. Steps

   - `2026-09-30-report.md` gets a one-line note at the top pointing to the new report.
1. **Inputs and code.**
   - `config.json`:
     - the planes get labels (the file names) and positions z = 1.27 x label;
     - the target gets `dot_diameter_mm: 1.5`;
     - the photo distances are recorded, for the plausibility check only.
   - `case.plane_files` returns (label, z, path). The tags, overlay titles and CSVs use the label for file names and
     z for positions.
   - `cylfit.py`:
     - fixed-plane models: M1b (delivered), M1c (column/row board), M1bt (+traverse tilt), M1bk (+k1), M1bw (+wall);
     - a one-parameter stage-scale block `zs` (z = s 2.54 k), for the check in item 4 of section 2, with its sigma;
     - the free-plane models stay for the comparison.
   - `figures.py`: a magnification figure replaces the plane-offsets figure. It shows the vertical and horizontal
     pitch against the stage position, the pinhole line, and the lens-front and camera-body references (item 1 of
     section 2).
2. **Calibration.** Run `run_calibration.py` with model M1b and the multi-start from scratch. The outputs keep their
   names in `X:\Shijie Zhong\Refraction\Processed\20260930\T0\cam1` (`camFile/cam1.txt` etc.).
3. **Tests**, rerun on the new model. Each `*_out.txt` stays beside its script.
   - T0, T0b (regression: the numpy tracer against C++, and the grouped Jacobian, now including `zs`).
   - T1, T2: their inputs are unchanged. I rerun them to confirm the same results; their two failed criteria stay as
     reported.
   - T3: synthetic recovery of M1b.
   - T4: held-out planes, even/odd from scratch and leave-one-plane-out, now predicted at the **stage position**.
   - T5: the model table (fixed-plane family, free-plane family, pinholes, f fixed at 9000 px), with held-out rms at
     the stage position and with each plane's own z.
   - T6: visibility, reload, and the centre bias for 1.5 mm dots.
   - T7: error budget against the tube constants, with the planes fixed, plus a stage scale of +-1 %.
   - D7: how far near-equal fits (traverse tilt, wall field) disagree on points inside the tube, which sets the
     realistic accuracy.
4. **Report.** `2026-10-01-report.md` covers what changed, the model choice and its evidence, the parameters, the
   tests and the remaining limits. The processed folder's `README.md` and the case `README.md` are updated.

## 4. Pass criteria

| test | passes if |
|---|---|
| T0, T0b | as in the first run |
| T3 | every parameter z-score below 3; held-out error below 0.05 px |
| T4 | held-out rms at the stage position below 1.5x the training rms, for both even/odd and leave-one-out; and the same with each plane's own z |
| T6 | one image per point; reload within 1e-6 px; centre bias below 0.02 px for 1.5 mm dots |
| delivered model | the fixed-plane fit with the best held-out rms at the stage position among the models pyOpenLPT can load (no wall field). M1b expected. |
