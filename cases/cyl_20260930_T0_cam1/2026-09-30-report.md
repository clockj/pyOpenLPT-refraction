## 2. Detection and indexing

**Detector** (`dots.py`).
- **Dots:** dark dots (darkness ~0.3) on a bright board (~115 grey levels, noise sigma ~2), normalised by a local
  background interpolated from the board alone.
- **What the dots look like:** they look laser-engraved, with a darker rim and horizontal streaks inside. Their
  top rim is darker than their bottom.
- **Astigmatism:** the tube makes the left/right edges blurry (10-90 % width ~12 px at z = 0) while the top/bottom
  edges stay sharp (~4 px). Both are blurred at z = 38 (T1b).
- **Primary centre:** an ellipse fitted to edge points taken along 96 rays, each at half the local edge height.
  For a blurred step this lies at the step whatever its height, so the uneven rim matters little.
- **Checks:** three other estimators (iso-contour centroid, saturated-weight centroid, steepest-descent edges) are
  kept as checks.

**Indexing** (`grid_index.py`). The origin dot is followed from plane to plane (it moves under 7 px per step,
against a pitch of 115-155 px). Each plane is indexed on its own affine lattice anchored there, and every dot is
checked against its nearest neighbour in the previous plane.
- **The column the user flagged:** the leftmost column hardly moves (u = 44.0-46.8 px in all 20 planes) and is
  i = 0 everywhere. The grid contracts towards it as the board approaches the camera. The extra column on the right
  enters as **i = 8, complete from z = 16 mm**, so no index ever shifts. The overlays show it (`overlays/`,
  `tests/t1_overlay_zXX.png`).

| test | result |
|---|---|
| T1 detection and indexing | **Indexing passes:** 48-54 usable dots per plane, 1029 in all; none missing, no duplicates, no tracking mismatch; lattice residual under 3 px. **Estimator agreement fails** (the plan's < 0.1 px): the steepest-descent check scatters by up to 0.7 px on blurred edges (T2: its noise is 0.1-0.23 px), and the iso-contour centre sits 0.4-0.8 px above the primary because of the darker top rim. |

## 3. Building the model

### 3.1 Starting: no external geometry was needed

- **Search:** 32 starting rigs (camera 400-1400 mm from the axis, axis depth -10..20 mm, two lateral camera
  positions), fitted with the fast back-projection residual. **All 32 converged to the same M1 solution**
  (back-projection RMS 1.061 px).
- **Refinement:** forward-projection fits of the 3 best starts agree to 1e-11 px.
- **Reproducibility:** the even-plane fit of T4a, run from scratch, lands within 2 mm and 76 px of the full fit.

### 3.2 Projections: pyOpenLPT's C++ model, with a verified fast copy inside the fits

The C++ projection takes 0.24 s per 1029 points, so a fit with many parameters would take hours. `cylfit.py`
therefore traces the same model in numpy: closed-form back-projection, and Newton for the forward projection. That
copy is used inside the fits and checked against the C++ (T0):
- **back-projection:** identical to rounding, origins within 1.6e-11 mm (2.5e-7 mm with k1 = 5, where the C++
  undistortion stops iterating sooner);
- **forward:** within 7.6e-6 px. That is the C++ solver's own tolerance: tightening it to 1e-14 brings the gap to
  1.9e-7 px.

Every reported residual is computed with the C++ camera, and at the solution the two agree to 2e-8 px. The grouped
Jacobian (plane offsets and board corrections perturbed together) matches the brute-force one to 1e-12 (T0b).

### 3.3 From M1 to M2b: what the residuals showed

| model | free | RMS (px) |
|---|---|---|
| M1 | pose, f, axis (11) | 1.275 |
| M2 (the plan's choice) | + 19 plane offsets | 0.859 |
| **M2b (delivered)** | **+ per-dot board corrections, affine part removed (102)** | **0.252** |
| M2c | + one X offset per column and one Y offset per row instead (11) | 0.484 |
| M2bw (diagnostic) | M2b + a ray deflection in the water that depends on the pixel column (16) | 0.202 (numpy tracer) |

M2 left 0.86 px, ten times the detection noise. The diagnoses traced it:
- **The pattern follows the dots** (D1). Removing each dot's mean residual over the 20 planes cuts the RMS from
  0.86 to 0.44 px, while no smooth function of image position comes close (a cubic leaves 0.60 px). It runs along
  whole columns: about +0.8 px for i = 2-3, -1.2 px for i = 5-6 and +0.9 px for i = 7-8 in u.
- **It is real** (D2). Computed separately from the even and the odd planes, the per-dot means correlate at
  **0.990 (u) and 0.995 (v)** and agree to 0.1 px.
- **Part of it is not on the board** (D3). Its strength falls steadily from 1.41x at z = 0 to 0.48x at z = 38 (in
  u). A board error would change only with the magnification (~10 %), and a detection bias would follow the blur,
  which is U-shaped in z. A steady fall fits an optical error fixed in the image, whose shift grows with the
  distance from the tube wall (111 mm at z = 0, 62 mm at z = 38). The view is nearly telecentric, so each pixel's
  ray always crosses the wall at the same place, and extrusion lines run along the axis, i.e. along the columns.
- **Mechanism** (D4, the same freedom for each hypothesis, u component of the M2 residual):

  | hypothesis | u residual left (px) |
  |---|---|
  | board error alone | 0.41 |
  | image-fixed pattern (lens) | 0.344 |
  | wall deflection x distance to the wall | 0.336 |
  | board + lens | 0.154 |
  | board + wall | **0.147** |

  So both act: board errors, and an image-fixed pattern along u that is somewhat better described as a deflection
  growing with the distance to the wall.
- **Not the sensor** (D5). The sensor's column pattern (a 32-column block structure of 0.2 %) moves edges by under
  0.01 px.

**M2b is delivered.**
- **Why it:** its board corrections keep the board's defects out of the camera and tube parameters, and they live
  in the world points, so the camera stays a plain PINCYL file. The wall field (M2bw) cannot be stored in PINCYL; it
  is reported as a diagnosis only.
- **What its residual is made of** (D1/D4 on M2b), in roughly equal parts:
  - an image-fixed pattern along u, ~0.14 px;
  - per-plane shifts, ~0.14 px, largest at the defocused ends of the traverse;
  - ~0.12 px left over.
- **Focus dominates the rest:** the residual per plane is U-shaped, ~0.1 px (u) and 0.04-0.07 px (v) at best focus
  (z = 18-24) and ~0.3 px at both ends (figures/residuals_per_plane.png).

## 5. What these data do not determine

- **The degeneracy.** Paraxially, a contraction of the grid about the tube axis looks the same as the board
  drifting obliquely to the camera-axis plane. Only higher-order effects separate "axis near column 0, traverse along
  the line of sight" from "axis mid-image, traverse 2.3 deg oblique". With a field of view of +-1.1 deg and 25 mm,
  against a tube 200 mm across, those effects are weak, and the board errors can mimic them.
- **The fitted geometry depends on the board model:**

  | model | RMS (px) | axis X (mm) | axis lean (deg) | camera X (mm) | view from -Z (deg) | traverse (mm) | f (px) |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | M2 | 0.859 | 2.3 | 2.31 | 7.0 | 0.95 | 48.72 | 42,319 |
  | M2c | 0.484 | 13.4 | 2.57 | 43.8 | 1.92 | 49.35 | 42,155 |
  | M2bw | 0.202 | 9.9 | 3.07 | 32.3 | 1.51 | 49.26 | 41,814 |
  | **M2b** | **0.252** | **19.3** | **0.06** | **62.7** | **2.80** | **48.80** | **41,416** |

- **Within M2b** (D6, the axis's lateral position held on a grid), the minimum is clear at X_a = 19-20 mm. The RMS
  rises by only +0.013 px at 15 mm and +0.026 px at 25 mm, while the camera moves from X = 49 to 81 mm and the view
  from 2.0 to 4.0 deg.
- **What it costs** (D7, projecting a 3-D grid filling the calibrated volume through each fit):
  - the fits that match the dots almost as well (axis held at 15 and 25 mm) differ from the delivered camera by
    **0.48 and 1.16 px RMS (max 1.0 and 2.6 px)**;
  - M2 differs by 0.70 px RMS, and M2bw's PINCYL part by 0.79 px;
  - their rays in the water through the image centre differ by only **0.02-0.05 deg**. The disagreement is mostly a
    lateral offset of 10-25 um at the object.
- **What is robust** across every model:

  | quantity | range |
  |---|---|
  | traverse | 48.7-49.4 mm |
  | f | 41.4-42.3 k px |
  | camera distance from the axis | 1.09-1.12 m |
  | z = 0 plane behind the axis | 12.8-13.4 mm |
  | axis tilt in the board plane | <= 0.35 deg |

**So:** within M2b the solution is stable and predicts held-out planes to 0.28 px (T4). For particles, which carry
no board correction, the accuracy is the model spread, about 0.5-1 px.

## 7. Findings for the experiment

1. **Check the stage.** The tube measures the traverse as 48.8 mm for the nominal 38 mm, in every model (48.7-49.4).
   - **Step size:** the mean step is 2.569 mm. That is within 1.1 % of 0.1 in (2.54 mm; 19 steps would make
     48.3 mm), which is inside the 1 % model spread and the 0.5 % index uncertainty. **The stage may step in tenths
     of an inch.**
   - **Scatter:** individual steps range from 2.16 to 2.88 mm, far beyond their 0.03-0.1 mm uncertainty
     (figures/plane_offsets.png).
   - **Until it is checked:** use the fitted plane positions (`calibration_points_fittedZ.csv`), not the readings.
2. **The lens is nearly telecentric.** f = 41,400 px puts the entrance pupil about 1.1 m from the tube while the lens
   front is 0.2-0.4 m away. That is consistent for a 180 mm telephoto macro at magnification 0.77 (pupil
   magnification ~0.3). Do not assume f = 180 mm / pixel size for close focus; calibrate in air at the working
   focus, or let the fit find f, as here.
3. **The board's dots are off their grid by up to 51 um.** For the next calibration, measure the board (a flatbed
   scan or a measuring microscope) or use a precision target (photo-etched glass). That removes most of the
   ambiguity of section 5.
4. **Give the geometry more leverage.** One camera seeing a 25 mm field near-telecentrically cannot separate the
   axis position from the traverse direction. Any of these would:
   - a known distance from the board to the tube axis or wall;
   - a wider field, reaching closer to the tube wall where the refraction is strongly nonlinear;
   - a second camera, or the board rotated or traversed in a second direction;
   - dots on a second, independent target.
5. **The tube wall may carry about 0.14 px of image-fixed error** (D4). To check it, image the board through a
   different part of the tube (rotate the tube about its axis) and see whether the pattern stays with the image or
   moves with the tube.
6. **Mind the focus.** The tube separates the horizontal and vertical foci. The residual triples from mid-traverse
   to the ends, where the dots defocus and the uneven rims bias the centres. A smaller aperture, or keeping the
   measurement volume near best focus, helps.
7. **Indices and radii.** They were held at their nominal values. T7 gives what each uncertainty costs.
