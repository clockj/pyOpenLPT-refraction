**What follows:**
- **Each recording needs its own calibration.** Do not use T0's camFiles with T2's images, or T2's with T0's. A
  calibration holds only while the cameras stay untouched.
- **T0 and T2 cannot be pooled** into one calibration as they are. The library has one pose per camera.
- **T2 will probably not fix f either.** It covers no more depth than T0, and depth is what separates f from distance
  (T0's report, section 1). T3 checks this.
