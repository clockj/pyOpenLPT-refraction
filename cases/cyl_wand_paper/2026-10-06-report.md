## E0b: the 27 pairs without a C++ image (`e0b_zero_images.py`)

- **Where:** every pair lies 90.06–94.37° in azimuth from its camera, at radii 2.0–37.9 mm.
- **What the reference finds:** one image each, on the near branch or the far branch, for a ray with 1 − |Λ|/Λmax of
  2.9×10⁻¹² to 7.8×10⁻⁹.
- **Why it is a miss:** that is where the two branches meet, as the ray grazes the circle through the point. The
  C++ reports "the point has no image".
- **How wide the blind band is:** much wider than the paper's own rounding limit near grazing (5.6×10⁻¹⁴, Section
  9.2.1). This is therefore a tolerance or margin in the C++ search, not the floating-point limit.
- **Rate:** about 3 per 100,000 pairs here.
- **The paper's claim it touches:** Section 9.2.1 says the C++ returned the same image lists as the Python reference
  on 4,000 points. That is true of those points, but not general. See reminder 2.
