## In short

- **The campaign's results stand for the paper's calibration.**
  - On the campaign's data, the paper's residual equals the library's to 3.4×10⁻¹¹ px (E1).
  - The two calibrations reach the same answer: the same observations, RMS within 1.2×10⁻⁹ relative, parameters
    within 1.7×10⁻⁵ σ (E2).
  - Today's library, with per-camera noise weights, moves T4's fits of 1 Oct by at most 0.038 Cramér–Rao σ (E2b).
- **The campaign's wand volumes are single-image.**
  - Every one of 907,000 point–camera pairs, from the data sets and from dense samples of the whole volume the
    generator draws from, has exactly one image (E0, E0c).
  - Wand ends reach 33.2 mm from the axis on the reference rig and 49.3 mm on the random rigs; r_c is 75.6 mm.
- **There is no C++ bug.** E0's first census, run with cameras from `cyl_model.to_cpp`, returned no image for 27 of the
  907,000 pairs.
  - **Where:** all 27 have a ray that grazes the point's own radius, where the near and far branches meet.
  - **Why:** `to_cpp` sets the projection's acceptance tolerance to 10⁻¹², but next to grazing, rounding limits the
    residual that any implementation reaches to 1.0×10⁻¹²–4.1×10⁻¹¹.
  - **The check:** at the projection's own default of 10⁻⁹, the C++ finds all 27 (E0c).
  - **The effect:** 7 of the 227,000 images in the campaign's data sets were therefore never generated. They are
    missing detections and change no result.
  - **Correction:** my first reading was a blind band in the C++ search. The step-by-step trace (`e0b_trace.py`)
    shows the C++ search finds every one of these rays, step for step with the Python reference.
- **E2 failed the criterion I wrote into the plan, and the criterion was wrong.**
  - I asked for 10⁻⁶ σ and 10⁻⁹ relative RMS, which is tighter than the optimizer resolves. Its stopping rule leaves
    about 10⁻⁵ σ along the flattest direction (T6, quoted in `cylinder_wand.bundle_adjust`).
  - The differences found, at most 1.7×10⁻⁵ σ and 1.2×10⁻⁹, are at that precision.
  - They are 600 times inside T5's own test for the same answer: 0.01 σ, and RMS within 10⁻⁶ px.
  - The scripts keep the criterion as planned and print FAIL. This report records why that verdict does not mean
    the fits differ.

## E0b and E0c: the 27 pairs without an image (`e0b_zero_images.py`, `e0b_trace.py`, `e0c_tolerance.py`)

- **Where (E0b):** every pair lies 90.06–94.37° in azimuth from its camera, at radii 2.0–37.9 mm.
  - The Python reference returns one image each, on the near or the far branch.
  - Its ray is within δ = 1 − |Λ|/Λmax of 2.9×10⁻¹² to 7.8×10⁻⁹ of grazing the circle through the point, where the
    two branches meet.
- **Step by step (E0b-trace).**
  - The C++ core (`refract_curved.project_cylinder`, at its default tolerance of 10⁻⁹) finds all 27.
  - Its classify and solve steps return the same seeds and the same solutions as the Python reference.
- **The cause (E0c).**
  - **Residuals:** the residual that step (4) reaches for these rays is 1.0×10⁻¹² to 4.1×10⁻¹¹ in both
    implementations. It follows the size of the rounding error in Φ next to the square-root edge at grazing,
    ε/√δ = 2.5×10⁻¹² to 1.3×10⁻¹⁰.
  - **Effect of the tolerance:** at 10⁻¹², the tolerance `cyl_model.to_cpp` gives its cameras, the core keeps 1 of
    the 27; at 10⁻⁹ it keeps all 27.
  - **E0's census again with cameras at 10⁻⁹:** every one of the 907,000 pairs has exactly one image.
- **What it touches.**
  - **Not the paper's projection claims.** The paper's projection and its C++/Python comparison use 10⁻⁹. Section
    9.2.1's sentence that the C++ "returned the same image lists as the Python reference" stands.
  - **Not the calibration.** The campaign's generator, `rigs.wand_frames`, builds its cameras with 10⁻¹², so 7 of the
    227,000 wand-end images of the data sets E0 regenerated were never made; those detections are simply missing. The
    calibration's own C++ cameras (the guard, and this case's residual start) also use `to_cpp` and would skip such a
    ray: the library's guard has no point to check there, and the paper's residual falls back to the library's
    Newton.
