**Timing protocol (C5).** The run is at below-normal priority, so the other session keeps the machine. A smoke run
measured our sphere search at 350 µs instead of 90 µs while the machine was loaded. Each call is therefore timed in
5 passes (the multi-start in 3), every method in turn at each point, and its fastest pass is kept. The results must
not change between passes (they did not).
