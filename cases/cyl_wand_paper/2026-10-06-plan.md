## 1. Goal

Show that the campaign's results are the results of the calibration that the paper describes, so that Section
9.2.2 can quote them. Two things must hold:

1. **Every wand end of the campaign's data lies in the single-image volume of every camera** (E0). Then the residual
   has one image to follow.
2. **There, the paper's residual equals the library's** (E1), **and so does the whole fit** (E2).
   - The paper's residual starts Newton at the image the paper's projection returns.
   - The library's starts at the detection.
   - Both refine the same image, so the two fits should agree to round-off.

## 2. Constraints

- **Writes only in this folder.** No library or test file of pyOpenLPT is edited. `cylinder_wand`, `cyl_model` and
  `tests/cylinder_wand/rigs.py` are imported read-only.
- **No run touches the other session's build.** The runs import a frozen copy of the compiled extension, set by
  `CYL_WAND_PAPER_EXT`.
  - The copy is `pyopenlpt.cp311-win_amd64.pyd` of 2026-10-05 22:12, SHA-256 `44486497…f1b`, kept outside the
    repository.
  - The other session can then rebuild while these runs hold the copy open: Windows locks a loaded `.pyd`.
  - Each run records the extension's hash, the pyOpenLPT commit, and the hashes of the imported library files at
    its start and its end. A change during a run is reported.
- **One core:** below-normal priority, `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1`, `PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1`, no pytest.

## 3. Design: `projection_fit.py`

- **`evaluate_by_projection`.** A copy of `cylinder_wand.evaluate` with one change: where Newton
  (`cyl_model.project`) starts.
  - It starts at the C++ `projectAll` image nearest the detection.
  - Where the C++ returns no image, it starts at the detection, as the library does. This is the fallback of the old
    plan's section 7: the C++ does not search outside the water.
  - Counters record the rows with 0, 1 or more C++ images, and the fallbacks.
- **`calibrate_by_projection(data, tube, **kw)`.** It calls `cylinder_wand.calibrate` with `guard_px=0`, inside a
  context manager that swaps in `evaluate_by_projection`.
  - The paper has no guard.
  - On the campaign's data the guard has nothing to drop anyway: every end lies inside 0.98 r_c, and the guard
    checks only beyond r_c (E0 confirms this).

## 4. Tests

| test | what | criterion |
|---|---|---|
| **E0** single-image volume | For each camera of the reference rig, and for each of the 50 T5 rigs, the C++ image count at every wand end of the data sets behind the paper's numbers, regenerated with the campaign's generators and seeds. It also covers a dense sample of the whole volume the generator can use: r < 0.98 r_c(0) and the cloud's heights ± L/2. It reports the largest wand-end radius and the largest \|C_z\| of their rays. | every count is 1 |
| **E1** equivalence | Residuals, `ok` flags and Jacobians of `evaluate_by_projection` against `cylinder_wand.evaluate`. They are compared on the reference rig (T4 seed 0) and on 10 T5 rigs, at the truth and at the campaign's 2°, 20 mm, 5 % start. | at the truth: residuals within 1e-8 px, Jacobians within 1e-6 relative. At the start: any difference reported with its cause |
| **E2** same fit | `calibrate_by_projection` against `cylinder_wand.calibrate(guard_px=0)` on the same data: T4 seeds 0–2, the same start as T4 (stages 3–4); T5 rigs 0–4 from scratch; the T9 data set with the wand length free. | parameters within 1e-6 of their σ; the same observations kept; RMS within 1e-9 relative |
| **E2b** the campaign's code version | The present library's fit, with per-camera weights, against T4's saved fits (`t4_fits.json`, equal weights) for seeds 0–2. | reported in units of σ, as evidence that the campaign's equal-weight results stand for the present estimator, alongside T12b |

**Then** `paper_numbers.py` reads the campaign's JSON outputs and E0–E2's results, and prints every number that
Section 9.2.2 and Supplement 1 quote, each with its source file.

**Time:** E0 about 10 min, E1 about 3 min, E2 about 30 min, on one core.
