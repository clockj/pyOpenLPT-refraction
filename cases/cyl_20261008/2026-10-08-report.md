## 1. Results in brief

- **T1 is tracked through all three cameras, with few ghosts.**
  - **The run:** the first 250 frames (62.5 ms at 4,000 frames/s), every particle matched in all three cameras:
    425 long tracks, 69,846 positions, about 360 particles per frame in long tracks at the end.
  - **Ghosts:** the control, with cam2 and cam3 shifted in time so that nothing can match, keeps 13 long tracks
    against the final run's 275 at frame 100, and 12 against 319 at frame 149: **at most 5 %**.
  - **The same tracking with cams 1-2 alone** reads at most 81 % ghosts (49 % by the swirl test), as on 7 Oct. The
    third camera turns it from mostly ghosts into nearly all real.
  - **The tracks:** they follow the swirl about the tube's axis (median speed 212 mm/s), with 5.5 µm of noise per
    frame. The cameras' residuals are 0.3-0.8 px, and the image widths match the shape model to 0.3 px.
- **What limits T1 is cam1.** Its faint, tilted line images give about 450 detections per frame, against 950 on cam2
  and 5,200 on cam3. A lower detection threshold (k 5) finds about half as many particles again (frames 0-149,
  section 8), at a ghost share of at most 9 % (k 6: at most 5 %).

- **The GUI** takes a pixel size per camera in the wand tab's camera table. With pixel sizes 20 / 20 / 13.5 µm it
  scales cam3's radius range and rod settings by 1.48. Without that, the settings that suit cams 1-2 kept none of
  cam3's test frames; with it, 19 of 24.
  - **Image lists lined up:** cameras that saved different image ranges, like this T0, are cut to the images every
    camera saved, using their Phantom headers.
  - **The camera table:** it now shows all its columns in the panel; before, Width and Height had hidden behind a
    scroll bar.
  - **Tests:** `tests/wand_gui_pixel_size/` (G1-G4c); without pixel sizes the detection is bitwise unchanged.
- **The calibration (T0)** fits the three cameras to a reprojection RMS of 0.703 px over 7,212 frames.
  - **Between cameras:** each camera's balls predicted from the other two miss by 1.1-2.2 px RMS.
  - **f:** as on 6-7 Oct, the wand does not determine f. The parts of the recording give f ±10 %, while
    f / distance agrees to 1 %.
  - **Sync:** cam3's 2,396-frame offset (from its header) is confirmed.

## 5. T1: the frames, the motion, the preprocessing

**The frames you chose.** Tracked: raw frames 0-249 of the 1,001, at 4,000 frames/s, copied locally
(`tests/t_copy_t1.py`).

**P0c, the motion** (window cross-correlation, `tests/p0c_t1_motion_out.txt`). The median image displacement per
frame is 0.35 px on cam1, 0.38 on cam2 and 1.03 on cam3. Its rule picks the frame step that keeps cam3 below 3 px:
**every frame**.

**S1, bright particles on black** (`tests/s1_preprocess.py`).
- **The recipe:** (the per-pixel 90th percentile of frames 0-249) − frame, minus its median over 15 px (cam3:
  23 px, its pixels being 1.48 times smaller), times a gain, clipped to 8 bits.
- **The gains** are 6 / 3 / 2, so the 8-bit noise is 10.2 / 5.9 / 5.4 counts.
- **The result:** the background sits at 0-2, and 46 % of the pixels are exactly 0. The particles reach 80-90 at the
  99.9th percentile.

**S1b, cam3 cropped** (`tests/s1b_crop_cam3.py`). The line detector's time grows with the image's area: 27 s for
cam3's 2048 × 1952 frame against 9 s for a 1280 × 800 one. STB detects once per IPR loop and frame, so the first
frames took 80 s each.
- **What the crop keeps:** cam3's rows 360-1575, every column. The points all three cameras see project into rows
  366-1561.
- **The cropped camFile** maps every point to the full camera's pixel shifted by 360 rows (to 2e-13 px).
- **Coordinates:** cam3's image coordinates in T1's results are in the crop; add 360 to the row for the full frame.

## 6. T1: three-camera matches and the shape model

**What the detector finds.** The line detector (k_detect 6) gives per frame 458 detections on cam1, 946 on cam2 and
5,262 on cam3. cam1's lines are faint and tilted with the tube's axis (−10.7° in its image, because cam1 looks
down), and most of them stay below the threshold. cam3's are round dots, and it sees roughly ten times more.

**S2, the shape model** (`tests/s2_shape_fit.py`). The 7 Oct model, fitted from three-camera matches. A pair of cam1
and cam2 detections near each other's epipolar curve becomes a match when a cam3 detection lies within 1.5 px of
the pair's projection.
- **The matches:** in the 25 held-out frames, 1,524 ± 46 true ones, about 61 per frame; random matches are about a
  fifth of the signal band.
- **Their distance from the cam1-cam2 curve:** 55 % within 1.0 px, 77 % within 1.5 px, 89 % within 2.0 px. The third
  camera sits at a median of 0.96 px.
- **The model (A for all three):**

  | camera | pupil A | focus z_f | base blur σ0 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | cam1 | 15.05 mm | 842 mm | 1.20 px |
  | cam2 | 12.20 mm | 916 mm | 1.05 px |
  | cam3 | 6.60 mm | 851 mm | 1.38 px |

  cam3's small pupil is why it sees dots.
- **The held-out width residuals:** 68 % within 0.28-0.37 px. The judgement reads FAIL only because cam1's vertical
  width drifts +0.24 px over the depths (4.8 standard errors); the 1.5 px criterion passes with room.
- **The depth cue:** all three widths must agree.
  - At 0.5 px / 20 % it keeps 50 % of the true matches and 3.1 % of the random ones.
  - At 0.8 px / 20 %, the setting used, 80 % and 11.4 %.
  - The most selective setting, 0.3 px / 25 %, keeps only 26 % of the true matches, too few with the third camera
    already removing most random matches.

**S0, the detector** (`tests/s0_detector_out.txt`, `tests/s0_detector_k_out.txt`).
- **The standard tracer finder** (ObjectFinder2D at 4 × the 8-bit noise) returns 9,250 / 17,222 / 52,773 local maxima
  per frame, mostly noise. Its background-subtracted count of true matches (1,579 per frame) is the difference of
  44,622 matches in the signal band and 42,949 expected at random, too uncertain to compare. With 53,000 cam3
  candidates per frame it is not usable for tracking.
- **The line detector's threshold:**

  | k_detect | detections cam1 / cam2 / cam3 | true three-camera matches per frame | random share of the signal band |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 6 (used) | 456 / 927 / 5,268 | 61 | 20 % |
  | 5 | 819 / 1,590 / 8,954 | 91 | 43 % |
  | 4 | 1,549 / 2,849 / 14,556 | 147 | 72 % |

  A lower threshold finds more true particles but more random matches; k 5 was tracked on frames 0-149 as a
  variant (section 8).

## 7. T1: tracking (S3) and its checks

- **Detection and model:** the line detector at k 6 on every camera; S2's shape model as the OTF, with regions from
  it; the depth cue requires all three widths to agree within 0.8 px or 20 %.
- **IPR:** two loops, every match in all three cameras, 2-D tolerance 2.0 px (then 3.0 px in loop 2), 3-D
  tolerance 2 voxels (0.06 mm).
- **Tracking:** voxel 0.03 mm; cam3 cropped (S1b); frames 0-249.
- **Time:** 30 s per frame, 2.1 h on a machine shared with another session's runs.

## 8. Three cameras against two, and a lower detection threshold

On frames 0-149, each run against its own control (`tests/t1_compare_out.txt`, the ghost controls):

| run | active long tracks at frame 50 / 100 | its control | ghost share (bound) | real tracks at frame 100 (bound) | swirl: tracks above 0.3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **three cameras, k 6 (final)** | 213 / 275 | 12 / 13 | 5.6 / 4.7 % | ≥ 262 | 10 % |
| cams 1-2 only, k 6 | 301 / 402 | 227 / 326 | 75 / 81 % | ≥ 76 | 32 % |
| three cameras, k 5 | 330 / 427 | 23 / 38 | 7.0 / 8.9 % | ≥ 389 | 12 % |

- **Two cameras:** about four in five long tracks may be ghosts by the count bound. On 8 Oct's synthetic cases this
  bound read 2-3 times high; the swirl test gives 49 %. Either way, most two-camera tracks are not real, and adding
  cam3 removes them.
- **The third camera** keeps every match real and costs little: the matches it rejects were mostly ghosts.
- **k 5** gives about 50 % more real tracks: at least 389 against 262 at frame 100, and 448 against 307 at frame
  149. The cost is a ghost share of at most 9 % (the swirl test: 16 %, k 6: 9 %) and slightly noisier positions
  (6.7 against 5.5 µm per frame). Rerunning k 5 on all 250 frames would deliver more particles at that cost.
