**Inputs:**

**Code:** the library and the GUI's glue, unchanged:

- `cylinder_wand_gui.write_outputs` for the files.

The case's own code only reads the images, applies the acceptance rules below, and runs the tests.

| step / test | what | judged by |
|---|---|---|
| S1 detection | All 10,640 frames of both cameras (about 25 min). A camera-frame is kept when it has two balls apart (the overlap guard), both fits have an edge RMS ≤ 1 px and an aspect 1.15–1.45, and both sizes are within 10 % of that camera's medians. Ball A is the small one. A frame is kept when both cameras keep it. | — |
| T1 detection check | Overlays of 40 random kept and 20 rejected camera-frames, which I look at. The centres' sensitivity to the rod settings (opening 91 / 95 / 101 px, mask 40 / 50 / 60 px) on 100 kept frames. Balls A and B followed from frame to frame. | no wrong ellipse among those I look at; the centres move by less than about 0.05 px with the settings; A and B never swap |
| S2 calibration | `cylinder_wand_gui.run` on all kept frames. This gives the delivered result: camFiles, report, page, warnings, run time. | it converges; every check on the page is explained |
| T2 residuals | Each camera's residuals: RMS, maps over the image (a pattern tied to the image), and the wand-length error frame by frame. | no pattern larger than about 0.1 px |
| T3 repeatability | The same calibration on each half and each quarter of the recording. The wand's poses differ between them. | every part within about 3 σ of the full fit (σ from the fit), and none in another minimum |
| T4 the scale check | The same with the wand length free (`fit_L`). | the fitted length agrees with your measurement within its uncertainty. The tube's radii set the scale on their own (to 0.02 % in T9's synthetic tests), so a difference means a wrong tube constant, water index, or wand length. |
| T5 k1 | The same with k1 free. | k1 is kept only if it clearly lowers the residuals |
| T6 frames | The calibration on frames thinned so that a ball moves at least 10 px between kept frames. Consecutive frames repeat almost the same pose. | the same answer within σ (if not, the thinned set is the honest one) |
| T7 cam1 against 30 Sep | If cam1's lens and focus are unchanged: its f against the dot board's 41,459 px (and 41,610 px in air), and its distance from the tube. | agreement within a few tenths of a per cent in f |

Expected time: about 25 min for the detection, then 5–10 min per fit. In all, about 1.5–2 h.

## 5. Risks

- **Two cameras only.** Their noise cannot be estimated separately, so the error bars assume equal noise.
- **Image-fixed errors** from the tube wall's imperfections: in the synthetic T11e, 0.07–0.15 px of such a pattern
  biased f by 0.2–0.5 % and made the error bars 3–4× too small. T3 and T4 are the checks for it.
- **The string and its fibres** sometimes cross a ball's outline. The robust fit and the edge-RMS rule handle most
  of these; T1 checks.
- **Defocus.** The wand swings in depth, and blur doubled the error in T13. The edge-RMS rule drops the worst
  frames.
- **The balls' real shape** (roundness, the joint with the rod). The rod's side is masked; the rest is unknown.
