D1, D2). You asked:

and, after reading the case plan: "go on and generate results and reports, update gui".

## The change

- **The camera table** of the wand tab gets a column "Pixel (µm)", empty by default.
- **Detection (D1):** with pixel sizes set, the Radius Range, Rod opening and Rod mask are in the first camera's
  pixels. A camera with pixels p gets them multiplied by p₁ / p, in Test Detect, Process Frames and the axis
  points. The overlap guard (3 px) stays. With no pixel sizes, or all equal, nothing is scaled.
- **f in mm:** the cylinder and flat-window results pages print each camera's f in mm with its own pixel size. The
  Tube Settings' single "Pixel size" box, which only did that, is removed.
- **Alignment (D2):** when every loaded camera's folder holds a Phantom header (`.chd`) and their first image numbers
  differ, the lists are cut to the images every camera saved, so that the i-th frames are simultaneous. Run
  20261008/T0 needs this: cam3 saved images 2396–13000, cams 1–2 saved 0–13000.

Code: `view.py`, `wand_calibration/wand_calibrator.py` (`scale_detect_settings`, `phantom_image_numbers`,
`align_by_image_numbers`, the workers' `cam_scales`), `curved_calibration/cylinder_wand_gui.py` and
`plate_wand_gui.py` (`pixel_mm` may be a list), and the user guide.
