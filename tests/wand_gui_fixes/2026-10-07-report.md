## 1. In short

- **"Bright on Dark" now detects bright balls** (B1). It had run as "Dark on Bright".
  - **The test:** frames of run T2 were inverted. On them "Bright on Dark" gives exactly the detections that "Dark on
    Bright" gives on the originals, in the reliable and ellipse modes; in the fast mode, within its own random spread.
- **The flat-window check is plate_wand's own** (B3). "Precalibrate to Check" runs plate_wand's start search and coarse
  fit (stages 1–3) in a separate process, about 40 s on 300 frames.
  - **What it shows:** the error table, the 3D View and a box with the RMS and each camera's f and window.
  - **What it does not do:** it writes no files.
  - **plate_wand's answers are bit-identical:** its start search became a module-level function for this.
- **Deleted frames come back** (B4).
  - **A new button,** "Restore Deleted Frames".
  - **New points start clean:** points loaded or detected later start with no frame deleted.
  - **Manual marks are kept:** a Del box clicked by hand keeps its state when a filter re-marks the rows.
- **Pause pauses the detection** (B5). Only the tasks already in flight still run: in a 10 s pause the last one started
  0.52 s in. Before the fix, 587 tasks started during the pause. The results equal an unpaused run, and the speed is
  unchanged.
- **The smaller fixes:**
  - **Num Cameras** keeps the remaining cameras' folders and settings (B6).
  - **The indices take four decimals,** with water at 1.333 (B7).
  - **All three Calibration panels fit their column** (B8). Before, they needed 391–460 px in a 358 px column, the
    Pinhole panel too.
  - **Pinhole camFiles** carry each camera's own image size (B9).
  - **The Pinhole error table** shows the model's red crosses (B10).
  - **The axis-point notes are right** (B2).
- **Found (not fixed):**
  - **B11:** the Pinhole model cannot calibrate cameras of different image sizes; it stops with an error.
  - **B12:** it also fails when the cameras in the points are not numbered 0, 1, 2, … without gaps.
  - **The "fast" detector is not repeatable:** two runs on the same image differ by up to 4.4 px.

## 4. Found on the way (not fixed: your decision)

### B11: the Pinhole model cannot calibrate cameras of different image sizes

**Fixed later the same day** (your OK): `2026-10-07-report-b11-b12.md`.

**What happens:** with one camera of 1024 × 768 px among cameras of 1280 × 800 px, Run Calibration stops with
"Calibration crashed: Initial guess is outside of provided bounds" (W9).

**Why:** `_run_optimization_pipeline` bounds every camera's principal point around `self.image_size`'s centre, which
is the first camera's:
- **stage 2:** ±0.1 px;
- **stage 4:** ±50 px.

Each camera starts at its own image centre (from its Width and Height), so a camera of another size starts outside
its bounds, and scipy refuses to start.

**Proposed fix:** bound each camera's principal point around its own centre.
- **The change:** in that one function, `_get_cam_setting(id)` for each camera instead of `self.image_size`, at its
  three places (stage 2, stage 4, and stage 4 when stages 1–3 are skipped).
- **Cameras of one size:** they get the same bounds as now, so their results stay bit-identical. A test would check
  that, and that the W9 rig then calibrates.

### B12: the Pinhole model fails when the cameras are not numbered 0, 1, 2, … without gaps

**Fixed later the same day** (your OK): `2026-10-07-report-b11-b12.md`.

**What happens:** points of cameras 0, 2 and 3 (a camera table of four rows, camera 1 without points) run the whole
calibration, then fail with "Calibration crashed: 1" (`f1_pinhole_ids.py`). The same points numbered 0, 1, 2
calibrate.

**Why:** `calibrate_wand` builds `cam_id_map = {camera id: internal index}`, but `_parse_results` reads it the other way,
`cam_id_map[internal index]`. That works only while the ids are 0…N-1.

**Proposed fix:** `_parse_results` inverts the map. A test would check the 0, 2, 3 case, and that the 0, 1, 2 results
are bit-identical.

### The "fast" detector is not repeatable

**Measured later the same day** (D2, `2026-10-07-report-b11-b12.md`, section 4): in air two runs differ by at most 0.04 px. The large differences are those on tube images.

`refine_circle_with_edges` fits each circle by RANSAC with numpy's unseeded random draws, in fresh worker processes, so
two runs on the same image differ: by up to 4.43 px on six T2 frames (W1). "reliable" and "ellipse" are repeatable.

**Possible fix:** seed each task from its frame and camera, which makes the fast mode repeatable. Its results would
change once (different draws).
