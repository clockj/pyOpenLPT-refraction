## 2. What is there now

**The GUI.** The Wand tab lives in `modules/camera_calibration/view.py` (8,171 lines).
- **Point Detection:** two circle detectors, "fast" and "reliable".
- **Calibration:** the models "Pinhole" and "Pinhole+Refraction", an error table with frame deletion, and a
  matplotlib 3-D view.
- **"Pinhole+Refraction" runs the old flat-window calibrator** (`refraction_wand_calibrator.py`), not the new
  `plate_wand` (see O5).
- Nothing in the GUI knows about cylinders.

**The library.** `cylinder_wand.calibrate(WandData, Tube)` needs four inputs: the detections, the wand length, the
image sizes and the tube.
- No camera input is needed: f comes from the wand.
- `cylinder_wand_io.write_result` writes three outputs: PINCYL camFiles (through the C++ writer), a result JSON and
  a text report.
- **Run time:** 20-60 s from scratch for 500 frames and 2-4 cameras.
- **Tests:** synthetic data only (T0-T13).

**Three things stand in the way of a plain hook-up.**
1. **The GUI's detectors cannot measure balls seen through the tube.**
   - Through the tube a ball's image is a 1.3 : 1 ellipse, and both GUI modes fit circles.
   - **In T13:** "fast" put both balls within 5 px in only 16-58 of 1,142 camera-frames, and "reliable" in none of
     286. Calibrations from their centres were off in f by a few per cent to tens of per cent.
   - **The fix exists:** the ellipse estimator with the rod's edge points left out gave 0.026 px RMS and f within
     0.07 %.
   - It lives in `tests/cylinder_wand/t13_ellipse.py`, not in the library.
2. **The rest of the GUI treats any camFile that is not PINPLATE as a plain pinhole.**
   - **Tracking settings** copies the calibration view's last result into the project's `camFile` folder. It knows
     only pinhole and PINPLATE results. After a cylinder run it could therefore write an older pinhole result over
     the PINCYL files.
   - **VSC** (`modules/vsc/vsc_service.py`) would treat PINCYL cameras as pinholes. It would save them as PINHOLE files
     in `camFile_VSC` and point `config.json` at them, so the tube would silently drop out of the tracking.
   - **The tracking view's overlays** and the settings' **volume estimate** project PINCYL cameras as pinholes. This
     affects the display only.
   - The C++ tracking itself takes PINCYL cameras: its camera classes are polymorphic.
3. **QtWebEngine is missing in `refract`.**
   - PySide6 6.11.2 there has the bindings, but Qt's WebEngine libraries are absent ("DLL load failed"). So today an
     HTML page cannot be shown inside the GUI.
   - conda-forge has `qt6-webengine` 6.11.2 for Windows, which matches the installed Qt (O3).
   - pip installs of PySide6 include it.

## 3. What I propose to build

### A. Detection: an "ellipse" mode

- **The control.** A third radio button, "ellipse", sits next to "fast" and "reliable". Its tooltip: for balls seen
  through a tube, slower than "fast".
- **Two settings appear when it is chosen.** Their defaults are T13's, for a 0.3 mm rod on cam1:
  - **"Rod opening (px)", default 25.** An opening that removes the rod before the balls are separated. It must be
    wider than the rod's image.
  - **"Rod mask (px)", default 18.** The edge points within this distance of the rod's line are left out. It should
    be about the rod's half-width plus twice its blur.
- **The estimator** (L2): T13's `detect_balls` and its 3 px overlap guard, copied unchanged into the library.
  - The background window grows with the Radius Range's maximum. It is never below T13's 101 px.
- **What it returns:** each ball's centre and its area-equivalent radius sqrt(ab).
  - Everything downstream therefore works unchanged: small and large ball by radius, the CSV, and the tables.
  - "Test Detect" shows it on any frame.
- `run_detection_task` gains the branch (L3). "fast" and "reliable" are untouched.

### B. Calibration: the model "Pinhole+Cylinder (PINCYL)"

**Settings.** Choosing the model shows a "Tube" group in place of the window group:
- **Media:**
  - camera side: Air (1.000) or other;
  - wall: Acrylic (1.49), Glass (1.52) or other;
  - inside: Water (1.333) or other.
- **Water is 1.333 here,** as in your tube case. The plate panel uses 1.33. The tube's radii set the scale, so a
  0.2 % error in n shows in f and in the wand length.
- **Size:** outer diameter and wall thickness in mm. The defaults are your tube: 201.61 mm (7.9375 in) and 3.175 mm
  (0.125 in).
- **Lens distortion:** None or k1, from the existing "Distortion params" box. k1+k2 is disabled for this model.
- **Options:**
  - "Fit the wand length". This checks the tube's size and indices (T9).
  - Noise weights: per camera (the default), per camera and ball, or equal.
  - "Thorough start search": carry 6 starts instead of 2. It is slower and meant for noisy data (T11b).
  - "Refit from the last result": skip the start search. This is for the delete-frames-and-rerun loop, and takes
    seconds.
  - Pixel size (µm), optional. It is used only to print f in mm.
- **The camera table:** the Focal column is greyed out with a tooltip, since this model finds f from the wand. Width
  and height are used.

**Run Calibration.** It asks for the output folder, as "Pinhole+Refraction" does, then:
1. The wand points, after the table's deletions, become uv[frame, camera, end]. End A is the small ball.
2. A worker thread runs `cylinder_wand.calibrate` with these settings.
   - The progress dialog shows the stage and the RMS of each iteration.
   - Stop ends the run at the next iteration (this needs L1).
3. The outputs go into the folder:
   - `camFile/cam0.txt`, `cam1.txt`, ...;
   - `calibration_result.json` and `calibration_report.txt`;
   - `calibration_data.json` (the residuals and the 3-D wand ends, for the page);
   - `calibration_report.html` (C).

**"Precalibrate to Check".** With this model it runs stages 1-3 only (the start from the data and the coarse fit),
which takes seconds, and fills the error table. It no longer runs the pinhole check, because a pinhole fit through a
tube shows the refraction as error.

**Axis points.** The custom axis direction is not applied for this model. The frame is the tube's: Y along the axis,
Z towards camera 0. The GUI says so if axis points are loaded.

### C. The results page (`calibration_report.html`)

**Format.**
- It is one self-contained file: inline SVG and a small canvas script, with no CDN libraries, so it works offline.
- It is dark like the GUI, with a light mode.
- Each figure shows its values on hover, with a data table under it.

**Contents:**
1. **Summary.**
   - What was used: cameras, frames and detections.
   - The reprojection RMS, and the wand-length error of a free triangulation.
   - The checks that fired (listed below), each with what to do.
2. **The rig from above,** looking down the tube.
   - The cameras at their fitted distances and azimuths.
   - Each camera's field of view, bent at the walls, and the wand ends.
   - The inner wall, and the fold radius. Beyond the fold radius a point can have two images.
3. **The rig in 3-D:** the tube, the cameras, their lines of sight and the wand ends. Drag to turn, scroll to zoom.
4. **Each camera's fit.**
   - One panel per camera: the sensor, with every detection's residual drawn magnified (the scale is shown) and
     coloured by size.
   - A table per camera:
     - f in px, and in mm when the pixel size is given;
     - distance from the axis, azimuth and height;
     - RMS, detections used out of the total, and sigma of f.
5. **The wand length.**
   - Its error per frame, against the wand's distance from the axis. A trend here means the tube's constants are off.
   - A histogram of the same errors.
6. **Noise and weights:** each camera's noise level, whether the weights were applied (or why not), and the starts
   tried.
7. **Inputs and files.**

**The checks, each with its evidence:**
- **Detections not from "ellipse".** T13: f was off by a few per cent to tens of per cent. The GUI knows the mode
  only for detections made in the same session.
- **RMS above 1 px.** The data are noisy: use the thorough start search (T11b).
- **A camera with less than 80 % of its detections used.**
- **Fewer than 200 frames used.** The campaign used 500.
- **Wand ends beyond the fold radius.** These can have double images. Keep the wand in the central volume (paper
  §9.1).
- **A fitted wand length more than 3 sigma from the nominal one.** The tube's constants are off (T9).

**The generator checks itself,** as the wand guide's does:
- every line of sight it draws meets its point within 1e-6 mm;
- every number it prints equals the JSON's.

### D. What the GUI shows after a run

- **The result box** (as now) adds the RMS, the wand-length error and each camera's f.
- **The 3-D View tab** draws the tube with the cameras and the points: the outer wall as a see-through surface, the
  inner wall as rings.
- **A new "Report" tab** sits on the left beside "2D Detection" and "3D View".
  - It holds a short summary, a per-camera table, and "Open report" and "Open folder" buttons. The page opens in your
    browser.
  - With O3 the page shows inside this tab instead.
- **The error table and the frame overlays** work as they do for the other models:
  - per frame and camera, the larger residual of the two balls;
  - the wand-length error;
  - red crosses at the fitted projections.
- **Tracking settings picks up the PINCYL camFiles.** The existing export hook (`export_refractive_camfiles_to_dir`)
  copies them, so `tracking_settings_view.py` needs no change.

## 5. Tests

They go in `tests/cylinder_wand_gui/`, with their results beside them.

**G0, the data path.**
- From the GUI's in-memory wand points, `uv_from_wand_points` must give the same arrays as
  `plate_wand_io.read_gui_csv` on the CSV the GUI exports. They must agree exactly, to the CSV's 3 decimals.
- A camera missing from a frame gives NaN.
- The small ball is end A.

**G1, the same answer as the library.** The rig is the campaign's reference rig, with 3 and 4 cameras, 500 frames,
0.1 px noise and fixed seeds.
- **Same cameras:** the GUI's runner and a direct `cylinder_wand.calibrate` call give the same cameras (1e-9
  relative).
- **The camFiles:** they load in C++, and the C++ projection of the fitted wand ends matches Python's within 1e-6 px.
- **Per-frame errors:** they equal an independent recomputation.
- **f:** within 3 sigma of the truth.
- **L1:** with `log_fits` off, the numbers and the log are identical to today's.

**G2, the detector.** The "ellipse" mode runs through `run_detection_task` on T13's rendered images (backlit and even
light).
- Its centres must equal T13b's `ellipse_rodmask`, which is the same code: 0.026 / 0.028 px RMS.
- The guard must remove T13b2's wrong centres of overlapping balls.
- "fast" and "reliable" must be byte-identical to today's on 20 frames.

**G3, the whole GUI path, offscreen.** The test renders image folders with T13's renderer: 3 cameras around your
tube, backlit, about 200 frames.
- **The path:** it loads them into the view, then runs Process All Frames ("ellipse"), "Pinhole+Cylinder" and Run
  Calibration, with the dialogs answered by the test.
- **The calibration:**
  - PINCYL camFiles are written and load in C++;
  - f is within 0.2 % of the truth (T13c reached 0.07 %);
  - the RMS is near the noise.
- **The GUI's outputs:**
  - every frame is in the error table;
  - the overlay crosses sit at the fitted projections;
  - the 3-D view draws without error, and the page is written.
- **Tracking settings:** its export copies the PINCYL files, not a pinhole result.
- **The same images with "fast":** the page's detector check fires.

**G4, the page.** It is built for G1 and G3.
- Every number on the page, parsed back, equals the JSON's.
- Its lines of sight meet their points within 1e-6 mm.
- The HTML is balanced.
- **Headless Chrome screenshots:** at 1,400 px and at 390 px wide, in dark and light mode.
  - No console errors.
  - No network request.

**G5, stop and bad input.**
- **Stop:** pressing it in stage 4 ends the run at the next iteration. The GUI comes back idle, and no camFile is
  written.
- **Bad input** gives a clear message, not a crash. The cases:

  - too few frames seen by every camera (the start needs at least 12);
  - missing image sizes;
  - a wall thicker than the radius.

## 8. Limits

- **The detector is tested on synthetic images only** (T13's renderer). There are no real wand images through the
  tube yet.
  - Real images may need other detector settings. "Test Detect" shows the result on any frame.
- **The model's scope:**
  - one tube for all cameras, seen through its side wall;
  - frames seen by every camera are needed for the start (at least 12);
  - 2-4 cameras were tested, 5 or more were not.
- **A known risk on real data (T11e).** An image-fixed error pattern, for example from the wall's flaws, biases f by
  0.2-0.5 %, and the error bars come out 3-4 times too small.
- **Not tested here:** tracking end to end with PINCYL cameras.
