## 1. The answers

**Your question: does the tracker filter out particles outside a given range?** Yes, by `view_volume_mm`.
- Stereo matching draws each epipolar line or curve only over the part of the line of sight inside the volume, and
  rejects a match whose triangulated point lies outside it.
- STB ends a track when its predicted position leaves the volume or fewer than two cameras can see it.

The filter acts on 3-D points only. The images of particles outside the volume stay in the frames. Point 4 shows
what they cost: recall, not ghosts.

**1. Detection on T1 (Part D): the gain is in the preprocessing.** The detector's own settings are near their best.
Template blur, deblending passes and the second pass change the true stereo pairs per frame by −34 % to +3 %. The
threshold trades true pairs against their share.

| T1, 10 frames | true pairs per frame | after the depth cue |
|---|---|---|
| today: 31 px median, k 6 | 190 ± 21 (5.6 % of the candidates) | 98 ± 5 (47 %) |
| 15 px median | 282 ± 24, +48 % (6.5 %) | 142 ± 6, +45 % (49 %) |
| 15 px median and k 5 | 448 ± 33, +136 % (5.4 %) | 224 ± 8, +128 % (45 %) |

- The 15 px median is the only setting that passes the plan's rule: at least 10 % more true pairs at no lower share.
- Combined with k = 5, it misses the rule by 0.1 point of share, within the error.
- **Where the truth is known, the gain is real.** On the synthetic case most like T1:
  - true pairs: +36 % with the 15 px median, +56 % with both;
  - tracking recall: from 0.14 to 0.22 (15 px median) and 0.24 (both), with fewer ghosts (13 % → 10–11 %).
- **T1 rerun with it (section 7, added on your request):** about twice the real tracks at the same ghost share, and
  nearly three times with k 5 as well, with slightly rougher tracks.

**2. More cameras (Part S).** Noise 8; the elevated cameras are 25° above and below the real pair.

| | 2 cameras | 3 cameras | 4 cameras |
|---|---|---|---|
| recall, particles seen by all of them | 0.71 | 0.88 | 0.77 |
| recall, particles seen by at least two | 0.71 | 0.79 | 0.54 |
| ghosts | 10.7 % | 5.5 % | 1.4 % |
| track purity | 0.89 | 0.94 | 0.99 |
| position error, RMS x / y / z | 18 / 8 / 16 µm | 18 / 8 / 16 µm | 12 / 6 / 11 µm |

- **A third camera** halves the ghosts and raises the recall.
- **A fourth** makes the tracks nearly pure and the positions better. With "one camera may be missing", though,
  every match then needs three cameras, so particles that only two cameras detect are lost.
- **The elevated cameras see larger, fainter images.** Their astigmatism is larger: 19 mm between the two focal
  depths against 12 mm. Median peak/noise at σ 8 is 3.4 (cam3) and 4.8 (cam4), against 7.8 and 6.3 for the real
  pair.
  - cam3, the shorter lens, also sees the most images (2,408 per frame) and detects only about a third of them
    (781). cam4 detects about two-thirds (1,138 of 1,722).
  - So full matches are few: at σ 8, about 110 per frame with all 3 cameras (over the three loops) and 60 with all
    4, out of about 1,000 particles.
  - Most of the extra cameras' gain comes from the camera pairs and triples, and from the shake step.
- **For a real rig:** an extra camera above or below should have a smaller aperture, or sit closer to the horizontal
  plane.

**3. Noise (Part S).** Recall of the particles all cameras see, at σ 4, 8 and 16:

| | σ 4 | σ 8 | σ 16 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 cameras | 0.70 (ghosts 11 %) | 0.71 (11 %) | 0.63 (8 %) |

| 4 cameras | 0.71 (1.0 %) | 0.77 (1.4 %) | 0.56 (1.1 %) |

- σ 4 and 8 track alike. At σ 4 the detector's threshold, which scales with the noise, lets in a few more faint

- σ 16 costs a tenth to a quarter of the recall, most with 4 cameras.
- T1's noise is about 8 in cam1 and 6 in cam2, so noise is not what limits T1.

**4. Particles outside the central region (Part O).** They cost recall but add no ghosts:

| particles outside (× the central density) | 2 cameras: recall / ghosts | 3 cameras | 4 cameras |
|---|---|---|---|
| none | 0.71 / 10.7 % | 0.88 / 5.5 % | 0.77 / 1.4 % |
| 0.5× | 0.64 / 10.2 % | 0.78 / 6.2 % | 0.53 / 0.4 % |
| 1× | 0.55 / 9.9 % | 0.67 / 6.7 % | 0.33 / 0.6 % |

- **Recall drops** because their out-of-focus images overlap the central particles' images: detection recall falls
  from 84 / 73 % to 73 / 63 % (cam1 / cam2), and centre errors grow a little.
- **No ghosts are added.** After the preprocessing, the outside particles leave few detections of their own, and the
  depth cue rejects the chance pairs they make.
- **The 4-camera rig loses most.** It needs three cameras per match, which compounds each camera's loss.

**5. Today's code** doesn't track line images, however many cameras it has: 2 cameras 0.18 recall / 91 % ghosts,
3 cameras 0.21 / 92 %, 4 cameras 0.10 / 78 %. On the same images, this work's settings give 0.71 / 11 %, 0.88 / 5.5 %
and 0.77 / 1.4 %.

**6. Why T1 tracks worse (Part G).** In order of effect:
1. **The shape model's error.** The depth cue keeps a true pair only when both image sizes agree with the model.
   - With T1's model exact, the cue keeps 75 % of true pairs and recall is 0.51.
   - At ±12.5 % error, 64 % and 0.41; at ±25 % error, 38 % and 0.18.
   - On T1 the cue keeps 52 %, so T1's model is off by about ±19 %.
   - Turning the cue off does not help: ghosts take over (72 %).
2. **The calibration error.** A 0.7 px error field, like T0's wand residuals, lowers recall from 0.71 to 0.57 and
   doubles the ghosts (19–22 %). T1's true matches are spread across the epipolar curves as in that case (58 % within
   1 px against 68 %).
3. **The particles outside the central region:** recall 0.71 → 0.55, no ghosts.
4. **T1's own image shapes:** larger, fainter images, T1's flux law and noise. With an exact model, recall
   0.71 → 0.51.

They compound:
- **All four together** (G-all) give recall 0.07–0.16 for a shape error of ±25 % or ±12.5 %.
- **A G-all matched to T1** (shape error ±19 %; T1's out-of-focus texture, detections per frame and measured noise)
  gives recall 0.13, ghosts 26 %, track purity 0.74.
- **It matches T1's truth-free statistics:**
  - the depth cue keeps 51 % of true pairs against T1's 52 %;
  - the spread across the epipolar curves is 66 % within 1 px against 58 %;
  - the detections per frame are 899 / 1,092 against 1,002 / 865.

**One gap remains.** T1's detections find a partner in the other camera half as often: 19 / 22 % against 42 / 35 %, and
190 against 380 true pairs per frame. So about half of T1's detections are of things only one camera sees, or are not
tracers. Candidates, each testable on T1 itself:
- static features (scratches, wall marks, bubbles), which the background removes only partly;
- more particles outside the common volume than a uniform filling gives;
- less common volume than the calibration says.

**T1's ghost share is likely 15–25 %, not 44–51 %.** The two estimators used on T1 were applied to three synthetic
cases where the truth is known:
- **The time-shifted control's count** reads 2.0–2.6 times the truth.
- **The swirl test** reads 1.5–3.6 times the truth. Real tracks also exceed its threshold 8–10 % of the time.
- **On the case matched to T1** they read 51 % and 61 % for a true 25 %.
- **T1's own counts estimate** is lower at the same frames (39 % at frame 40 against 51 %). Scaled by the synthetic
  cases' ratio of truth to estimate, 0.39–0.49, it puts T1 at about 15–25 %.

## 2. What was built, and the changes from the plan

**Rigs** (`make_rigs.py`): cam1 and cam2 are T0's tracking camFiles.
- cam3 is cam2's lens (f 28,306 px) at its distance, 829 mm, moved to azimuth 30° and 25° above.
- cam4 is cam1's lens (f 48,494 px, 1,187 mm) at azimuth 30°, 25° below.
- Both look at the centre of the region both real cameras see, T = (11.05, 1.47, −7.77) mm. The optical axis is
  set along T's ray in air, so T images at the principal point (to 1e-8 px). Aiming in air would miss by 468 and
  554 px: the tube bends the rays.
- Every point of the view volume a camera sees is certified single-image (100 % of 3,838–4,698 points per camera).
- Coverage of T1's view volume:
  - both real cameras see 60.2 % of it;
  - 3 cameras: 78.3 % seen by at least two, 56.3 % by all three;
  - 4 cameras: 82.2 % by at least two, 67.9 % by at least three, 47.0 % by all four.
- **The epipolar lines cross.** In cam1, cam2's lines run at 0.1° (horizontal, along the images' stretch), cam3's at
  −28° and cam4's at +25°. In cam3, cam4's lines are vertical, across the stretch.

**Renders** (`synth_study.py`, `data/main`): one population for all rigs and sets, moving with the swirl of r5_lines
(median image step 1.5 px), 40 frames.
- **Central layer** (r < 30 mm): 0.07 particles per mm³, 4,118 particles seen by a camera. Images per frame: cam1
  1,743, cam2 2,374, cam3 2,408, cam4 1,722 (r5_lines: 1,696 and 2,154). About 1,055 particles per frame lie in the
  view volume and are seen by both real cameras.
- **Two outside layers** (30 ≤ r < 97.6 mm) at half that density each: 12,930 and 13,059 particles, about
  2,200–3,300 more images per camera each.
- **Shapes:** the clean model as r5_lines (A 15 mm, σ0 1 px, flux 2,500 × U(0.6, 1.4)), or T1's fitted model.
- **The error field:** T0's wand-residual pattern (a quadratic fit to its cell means) scaled to 0.7 px RMS, moving
  the images. The tracker keeps the true cameras.
- **Noise:** one standard-normal field per camera and frame, scaled by σ, so the sets differ only in σ.

**Changes from the plan,** each found while building:
1. **Focus at least confusion.** The r5 rule (8 mm before the tangential focus) left the elevated cameras' images too
   faint: median peak/noise 2.2 and 3.2 at σ 8. Every camera is now focused at the circle of least confusion of the
   central particles, 1/z_f = median of (1/z_tan + 1/z_sag)/2.
   - For cam1 and cam2 that is 12 mm before z_tan; T1's fitted focus is 11 mm before.
   - For the elevated cameras it is 19 mm before.
   - Median peak/noise at σ 8: cam1 7.8, cam2 6.3, cam3 3.4, cam4 4.8.
2. **T1's preprocessing applied to every set.** A first one-frame trial of G-all (`before_hp/`) showed the
   out-of-focus particles' smooth background (about 50 counts in cam2) stopping the detector: cam2's detections fell
   from 281 to 1 as the density rose. On T1, S1's median removes that background. Every set therefore goes through
   S1's high-pass:
   - minus the 31 px median, at the raw scale where the noise is 1.4 counts as on T1;
   - recentred, rounded and clipped.
3. **T1's flux law held beyond its measured range.** It grows with the image's size (cam2's exponent is 1.16) and was
   fitted on detected objects, which favours bright large ones. G-shape and G-all hold it at |b| + |c| = 50 px. The
   case matched to T1 holds it at 8 px, which brings the measured noise and detections near T1's (below).
4. **The runs added** are listed in the plan's section 7. Each was added because a result needed explaining:
   - the three diagnostics of G-shape (cue off; exact model; half the error);
   - G-all at half the error;
   - D1's combined setting;
   - the tracking checks of the 15 px median;
   - the case matched to T1.

**Scoring** (`eval_study.py`): T1's view volume for every rig, a match within 0.2 mm.
- Recall is counted over the particles inside the volume: those seen by all of the run's cameras, by at least
  n − 1, and by at least two.
- Ghosts are reconstructions near no particle that two cameras see.

**Tracker settings:** T1's final run:
- the line detector, told each camera's noise;
- the shape model's OTF with ROIs;
- the depth cue at 0.8 px / 20 %;
- voxel 0.03 mm, 3 IPR loops, 3-D tolerance 2 voxels, 2-D tolerance 1.0 px (1.5 px with the error field);
- with 3 and 4 cameras, 2 more loops with one camera allowed missing.

## 6. Limitations

- **Double images are rendered once.** Particles more than 75.6 mm from the axis can have two images in a camera;
  the render draws one, the primary. They are all out of focus and outside the tracked region.
- **The error field is an image-plane field.** It is shaped like T0's wand residuals and scaled to 0.7 px RMS. A real
  calibration error also varies with depth.
- **The flux law of large blobs is uncertain.** Holding it at 50 px makes G-all's cam2 texture far stronger than T1's
  (measured noise 9.7 against 5.9). Holding it at 8 px matches T1. The out-of-focus particles on T1 are fainter than
  the law extrapolates.
- **The run times are not comparable.** They were measured with two to four runs sharing the 32 cores.
- **T1's ghost share** is inferred from the estimators' bias on synthetic cases, not measured.
