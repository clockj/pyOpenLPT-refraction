# Self-calibration of T1 from its own tracks: the cameras (VSC) and the shape model — plan

8 October 2026. **Status: plan, awaiting your approval.**

You asked:

> will vsc (using reliable long tracks to further optimize camera parameters) help?

> the current vsc sometimes has too restrict criterion on particle density or spatial distribution, so do not fully
> rely on that.

## 1. Facts first

**What limits T1.** The follow-up study (`tests/cylinder_stb_study/2026-10-08-report.md`) ranks the shape model's
error first and the calibration error second:
- **True pairs near the curve:** 58 % of T1's true pairs lie within 1 px of the epipolar curve (estimated). The
  synthetic case with a 0.7 px calibration error has 68 %, and the same case with exact cameras 88 % (study G1).
- **The cost of that error:** recall 0.705 → 0.571 and ghosts 10.7 % → 19.4 % (study G2, 2-D tolerance 1.0 px).
- **The shape model's error (about ±19 %)** leaves the depth cue only 52 % of T1's true pairs. With exact cameras
  but a ±25 % shape error, recall was 0.18; with the model exact, 0.51.

**The current VSC can't run on T1, and its selection would drop most of T1's points** (`src/srcSTB/VSC.cpp`):
- **Camera model:** it optimises PINHOLE cameras only (12 parameters each: pose, f, principal point, k1, k2), and
  skips any other type, so it can't touch the tube cameras.
- **Point selection:** a point is kept only if
  - its track spans at least 30 frames;
  - every camera sees it;
  - a detection lies within the object's radius of its projection;
  - that detection is isolated: no other within the two radii + 1 px. With about 1,000 line images per camera,
    most lines touch a neighbour.
- **Spatial balance:** each voxel is capped at twice the average count, and the fit waits for 5,000 points sampled
  every 10th frame.

As you say, this plan doesn't rely on it. It builds its own selection and estimator, in Python first, and borrows
only VSC's idea.

**What two cameras can see.** Tracks show only the error across the epipolar curves: the two cameras' residuals
point apart. That is the part matching needs. The part along the curves only shifts depth; it stays as T0 left it.

**The data.** The 15 px run, T1's result since 8 Oct (`cases/cyl_20261007/data/runs/s3_final_m15`): 1,228 long
tracks, 112,383 positions, about 570 particles per frame from frame 150. Ghosts are a minority, likely 15–25 %.

## 2. Approach

Two corrections, each estimated from the tracks and tested without changing the library. Only one that helps would
then be built in, with your OK.
- **C, the cameras:** a smooth correction field per camera in image coordinates, a "disparity map" (the original
  VSC of Wieneke 2008). It says where a camera's images sit relative to where the model projects them.
  - **Model-free:** it also absorbs errors the PINCYL model can't express, such as the tube's position or lens
    distortion.
  - **No gauge problem:** it needs no scale or f, so T0's f / distance valley doesn't return.
- **M, the shape model:** refit the line-image model from the tracked particles' measured widths at their known
  depths. The parameters are the pupil A, the focus z_f and the base blur σ0 per camera, plus a term for the depth
  trend T1-c found (up to 0.9 px).

**The point selection, instead of VSC's:**
- **Every point of every long track** of at least 10 frames: no isolation test, no voxel cap, every frame.
- **Robust statistics instead of strict filters.** Ghosts and blended detections give random residuals, not a
  smooth pattern, so per-region medians and a Huber fit ignore them.
- **A smooth field:** a low-order polynomial or a thin-plate spline in image coordinates, regularised. Sparse
  regions borrow from their neighbours instead of being dropped. The fit reports where it is constrained (a
  coverage map) and holds its edge values outside the data.
- **Held out:** every fit is made on half the frames (or tracks) and judged on the other half.

## 3. Tests

**C, the cameras:**

| step | what | judged by |
|---|---|---|
| V0 | **The pattern.** On every 5th frame, run the line detector on both cameras and pair each long-track point with its detections. Then compute: the residuals du, dv per camera (detection − the track's projection); the signed offset across the epipolar curve; maps per camera by region; the same maps from the first and the second half of the frames; and the offsets binned by depth. Also a truth-free estimate that uses no tracks: per region, the background-subtracted peak of all pairs' offsets (as T1-a), to show ghosts don't drive it. | **A real pattern:** the regional medians' RMS ≥ 0.3 px, with the halves agreeing (correlation ≥ 0.7) and the two estimates agreeing. **Stop if** the offsets depend on depth more than on image position: an image field can't fix that (a blurred line's centre may sit off the chief ray). |
| V1 | **The benefit, truth-free.** Fit the field on half the frames and apply it to the other half's detections. Then compute S2's pair statistics (as the study's D1) at tolerances 0.5, 0.75, 1.0 and 1.5 px. | **Sharper true pairs:** the share within 1 px rises from 58 % towards 80 %; at a tighter tolerance the true pairs are kept and the random ones fall in proportion. |
| V2 | **Where the truth is known:** G-all matched to T1 (`Gallm`), whose 0.7 px error field is known. (a) The same set rendered without the error: the most a correction can give. (b) The field fitted from Gallm's own tracks by V0's method, compared with the true field. (c) Gallm tracked on images warped by the fitted field. | (b) recovers the true field within about 0.2 px RMS, and (c) comes close to (a). The gain of (c) over Gallm (recall 0.131, ghosts 26.2 %) is the gain to expect on T1. |
| V3 | **End to end on T1, with no library change.** Warp the 15 px frames by the fitted fields, so each camera's images move onto its model; the detector is told the noise measured on the warped frames. Then run S3 final and control at 2-D tolerances 1.5 and 1.0 px, T1's checks, and r1's comparison against the 15 px run. | **More real tracks** by the counts estimate (it follows the true gain), with the ghost share no higher and smaller residuals (T1-a). |

**M, the shape model:**

| step | what | judged by |
|---|---|---|
| M0 | **The data.** The long tracks' widths against the model at their depths (T1-c, every 5th frame). Caveat: these tracks passed the current depth cue (0.8 px / 20 %), so their widths are censored towards the current model. M0 therefore also uses a T1 run with the cue loosened (1.5 px / 40 %), keeping only long, swirl-consistent tracks. | — |
| M1 | **The refit,** on half the tracks: A, z_f and σ0 per camera, plus a depth term. The held-out depth cue is then scored with S2's statistics: true / random pairs kept at the tracker's tolerances, against today's 52 % / 3.5 %. | **More true pairs kept** (≥ 65 %) with no more random ones. **Stop if** the error is per-particle scatter: a refit can't remove that. |
| M2 | If M1 helps: rerun T1 with the refitted OTF table (data only, no code change), then the checks and the comparison. | as V3 |

**Warping in V2c and V3.** A smooth sub-pixel warp moves each image by the field and lets the unchanged camera
files be tested without a library change. Its bicubic interpolation smooths the images slightly. V2c measures that
effect where the truth is known, and the detector is told the noise measured after the warp.

## 4. Then, with your OK

- **C in the library:** an optional per-camera correction field in the camera model.
  - **How it applies:** added to every projection, and removed from a pixel before its line of sight is traced.
  - **Where:** in the camera base class, so it works for every camera type. It is off by default, so existing
    cases stay bit-identical.
  - **Or, by D1:** refit T1's PINCYL camFiles from T0's wand points and T1's tracks together.
- **M:** a new OTF table and depth-cue tolerances (data).
- **Then:** rerun T1 with whichever helps, and update X: and the reports as before.

## 5. Decisions (my recommendation first)

| # | decision | recommendation | alternative |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | the camera correction's form | a smooth image-space field per camera: model-free, no scale or f problem | refit the PINCYL parameters from T0's wand points and T1's tracks together: one model, but it can't capture what the model can't express, and the f / distance valley returns |
| D2 | the shape model | include M: it is the larger limit | the cameras only |
| D3 | end-to-end tests | warped images, no library change until the tests show a gain | build the library feature first |
| D4 | the tighter tolerance in V3 | 1.0 px (beside 1.5) | also 0.75 px |
